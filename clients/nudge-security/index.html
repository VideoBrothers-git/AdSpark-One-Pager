What you get, and when

Five things, six weeks.

Six weeks, start to decision point: the Ad Suite, or the Advertising System.

  1. Discovery and Onboarding Week 1

    About 30 minutes of your time — a questionnaire on your brand, your audience, and the problem you solve — then a kickoff call.

  2. Campaign Directions Weeks 2–3

    Key message plus creative concepts, presented to Nudge Security within about a week or two. Directions are picked, not edited: they're deliberately open so you're choosing a path, not wordsmithing one. We judge them partly on legs — how far one idea stretches across channels.

  3. Scripts Weeks 3–4

    One 30-second Hero on the main problem, and three 15-second Pillars on the same problem from different angles. The Pillars are not cutdowns — nobody wants a shorter version of something they've already seen — each is its own ad, written and shot on its own. Two or three rounds; most lock on the second.

  4. Media Plan and Executive Presentation Weeks 5–6 Every tier

    The go-to-market for running the campaign across channels, built by Elite Media Group alongside the creative — informed by it, and informing it. It's a courtesy at this stage, regardless of which media solutions you end up using — there's no media commitment yet. Delivered as one presentation your leadership can approve in a single sitting.

  5. Production Lookbook Weeks 5–6 $10,000 tier

    Mood, wardrobe, props, tone, characters, environments — everything needed to visualize and shoot the campaign.

The biggest lever on speed is your side of the table — how fast feedback comes back. Rush schedules exist, are rare, cost a lot more, and usually aren't worth it; we'll say so.

What you get

The Standard Ad Suite plan, five things.

Every Ad Suite takes around 8 weeks from Ad Spark sign-off. This is where the production and everything that comes with it happens. The client owns everything made for them; talent agreements are pre-negotiated so renewing usage past two years is easy and affordable.

  1. Pre-production Weeks 1–4

    Casting, locations, wardrobe and prop pulls — everything specified in the Ad Spark lookbook gets built out and locked before anyone shows up on set.

  2. Production days Week 4–5

    One or two days on set depending on the plan. Days on set, cast size, locations, and set builds are what move a quote between $50,000 and $100,000 — the idea doesn't move it, a great script costs the same to shoot as a mediocre one.

  3. One Hero, three Pillars Week 4–5

    The 30-second Hero and three 15-second Pillars locked back in Ad Spark, all shot on set. The Pillars are not cutdowns — each is its own ad, written and shot on its own.

  4. Every social version Final delivery Standard plan and up

    With and without subtitles, in every format you'll run — 1:1, 16:9, and 9:16 — with a recommendation on where each version plays best.

  5. Photos from set Week 4–5 Standard plan and up

    Real stills from the shoot day for the site, decks, and paid social — not stock, not renders.

The amount of content and the specific formula may be adjusted during the Ad Spark process — the point is to be flexible for the best results for the brand.
